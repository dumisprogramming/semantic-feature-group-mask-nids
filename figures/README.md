# Manuscript figure descriptions

All figures are generated by `scripts/generate_figures.py` from committed aggregate CSV files. Both vector PDF and 300-dpi PNG formats are supplied.

1. **Figure 1 — Source-only development and frozen evaluation protocol.** Pairwise masks and target labels are excluded from model and policy construction.
2. **Figure 2 — Condition-level F1.** All complete, four single-loss and six unseen pairwise-loss conditions are displayed for baseline, mask augmentation and augmentation plus indicators. Bars are five-seed means; error bars are seed-level standard deviations.
3. **Figure 3 — Component ablation.** Pairwise-average F1 for baseline, indicators only, mask augmentation and augmentation plus indicators. Error bars are seed-level standard deviations.
4. **Figure 4 — Condition-level effect heterogeneity.** Five-seed mean F1 change for augmentation-only relative to baseline under each unseen pairwise loss.
5. **Figure 5 — Selective prediction.** Source-selected risk–coverage and accepted attack-class F1 curves for the three manuscript models on source and external-target pairwise losses.

The Random Forest model-family sensitivity analysis is reported in manuscript Table 10 rather than substituted for any of Figures 1–5.
